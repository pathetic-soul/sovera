## Findings

1. General wall loss is consistent with the design corrosion allowance across
   the majority of grid points.
2. **Accelerated localised thinning at the shell mid-section**, with a maximum loss of
   0.2 mm at grid S1.
3. The pattern is consistent with sulphidic corrosion following the modified McConomy trend. Supporting evidence: sulphur content at 3.2 wt% against a design basis of 1.8 wt%.
4. MPI on accessible welds: no linear indications.
5. Minimum measured thickness anywhere on the storage tank:
   **7.2 mm at grid S1**, against a retirement limit of
   5.0 mm.

## Recommendation

1. Recalculate remaining life for grid S1 and set the next inspection
   interval to the lesser of half the remaining life and 10 years, per
   API 653.
2. Raise an MOC to address the corrosion driver identified in finding 3.
3. Repeat the UT survey on the shell mid-section grids after 24 months.
