## Findings

1. General wall loss across the vessel is uniform and low (0.2–0.6 mm over the
   interval), consistent with the design corrosion allowance.
2. **Accelerated localised thinning along the bottom quadrant** (grids S6, S7,
   S8), with a maximum loss of 1.7 mm at S7. The pattern is a continuous band
   at the 6 o'clock position running the length of the shell.
3. The thinning band is consistent with an aqueous chloride water-drop
   mechanism at the vapour/water interface. Overhead water draw analysis for
   Q3-2024 records pH 5.2 against a target of 6.0–6.5.
4. MPI on both circumferential head-to-shell seams: no linear indications.
5. No coating was applied internally at the 2019 turnaround.
6. Minimum measured thickness anywhere on the vessel: **8.9 mm at grid S7**,
   against a retirement limit of 7.4 mm.

## Recommendation

1. Recalculate remaining life for grid S7 and set the next internal inspection
   interval to the lesser of half the remaining life and 10 years, per API 510
   clause 7.2.
2. Raise an MOC to add continuous overhead water pH control with a target of
   6.0–6.5; the current excursion to 5.2 is the driver of the S7 corrosion rate.
3. Apply an internal coating to the bottom quadrant at the next shutdown.
4. Repeat the UT survey on the bottom quadrant grids after 24 months, without
   waiting for the full internal inspection.
