## Findings

1. General wall loss is consistent with the design corrosion allowance across
   the majority of grid points.
2. **Accelerated localised thinning at the 6 o'clock position**, with a maximum loss of
   2.0 mm at grid S11.
3. The pattern is consistent with CO2 corrosion under a stagnant water layer. Supporting evidence: no continuous water draw-off during the last run.
4. MPI on accessible welds: no linear indications.
5. Minimum measured thickness anywhere on the column:
   **12.5 mm at grid S11**, against a retirement limit of
   11.9 mm.

## Recommendation

1. Recalculate remaining life for grid S11 and set the next inspection
   interval to the lesser of half the remaining life and 10 years, per
   API 510.
2. Raise an MOC to address the corrosion driver identified in finding 3.
3. Repeat the UT survey on the 6 o'clock position grids after 24 months.
