## Findings

1. General wall loss is consistent with the design corrosion allowance across
   the majority of grid points.
2. **Accelerated localised thinning at the downstream elbow**, with a maximum loss of
   0.2 mm at grid S6.
3. The pattern is consistent with erosion-corrosion downstream of the control valve. Supporting evidence: measured velocity 7.1 m/s against an erosional limit of 4.5 m/s.
4. MPI on accessible welds: no linear indications.
5. Minimum measured thickness anywhere on the column:
   **15.3 mm at grid S6**, against a retirement limit of
   12.7 mm.

## Recommendation

1. Recalculate remaining life for grid S6 and set the next inspection
   interval to the lesser of half the remaining life and 10 years, per
   API 510.
2. Raise an MOC to address the corrosion driver identified in finding 3.
3. Repeat the UT survey on the downstream elbow grids after 24 months.
