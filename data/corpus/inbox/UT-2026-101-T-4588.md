## Findings

1. General wall loss is consistent with the design corrosion allowance across
   the majority of grid points.
2. **Accelerated localised thinning at the bottom quadrant**, with a maximum loss of
   0.2 mm at grid S2.
3. The pattern is consistent with aqueous chloride water-drop attack at the vapour/water interface. Supporting evidence: overhead water draw pH 5.4 against a target of 6.0-6.5.
4. MPI on accessible welds: no linear indications.
5. Minimum measured thickness anywhere on the storage tank:
   **7.4 mm at grid S4**, against a retirement limit of
   4.3 mm.

## Recommendation

1. Recalculate remaining life for grid S4 and set the next inspection
   interval to the lesser of half the remaining life and 10 years, per
   API 653.
2. Raise an MOC to address the corrosion driver identified in finding 3.
3. Repeat the UT survey on the bottom quadrant grids after 24 months.
