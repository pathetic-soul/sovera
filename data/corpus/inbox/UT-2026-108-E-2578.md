## Findings

1. General wall loss is consistent with the design corrosion allowance across
   the majority of grid points.
2. **Accelerated localised thinning at the outlet nozzle region**, with a maximum loss of
   2.3 mm at grid S11.
3. The pattern is consistent with naphthenic acid corrosion at elevated temperature. Supporting evidence: TAN of the processed crude at 1.57 mg KOH/g against a design basis of 0.5.
4. MPI on accessible welds: no linear indications.
5. Minimum measured thickness anywhere on the shell-and-tube exchanger:
   **6.4 mm at grid S11**, against a retirement limit of
   5.8 mm.

## Recommendation

1. Recalculate remaining life for grid S11 and set the next inspection
   interval to the lesser of half the remaining life and 10 years, per
   API 510.
2. Raise an MOC to address the corrosion driver identified in finding 3.
3. Repeat the UT survey on the outlet nozzle region grids after 24 months.
